**PantryPal** is a premium, 100% offline kitchen management app for Android. Built to help you track your pantry inventory, monitor expiration dates, and discover recipes based on the ingredients you already have. 

Cook smarter. Waste less. Eat better.

---

## ✨ Features

*   🚦 **Expiry Radar:** Visual dashboard showing what needs to be eaten today, soon, and what's fresh.
*   🧑‍🍳 **Smart Recipe Matcher:** Select the ingredients you have, and PantryPal will find recipes you can cook right now (even if you're missing just 1 ingredient).
*   🛒 **Grocery List:** Auto-generate grocery lists for missing ingredients or depleted items.
*   🍳 **"I Cooked This":** Deduct used ingredients from your pantry with a single tap.
*   🔔 **Expiry Notifications:** Daily reminders via WorkManager so no food goes to waste.
*   🎨 **Premium UI/UX:** "Premium Culinary" theme with Sage Green and Terracotta accents.
*   🔒 **100% Offline & Private:** Room DB ensures all your data stays on your device.
*   💰 **Ad-Aware:** A single, non-intrusive AdMob banner.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Language:** Kotlin
*   **UI:** Jetpack Compose (Material 3)
*   **Local Database:** Room DB (with KSP)
*   **Background Tasks:** WorkManager
*   **Architecture:** MVVM + Clean Architecture
*   **Monetization:** Google AdMob
